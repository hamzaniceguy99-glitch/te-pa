What we provide

TE-PA provides construction, demolition, masonry and sanitary work carried out by TE-PA, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0689.779.569. DEMOLITION FOLLOW-UP: in the Flemish Region a demolition follow-up plan must be drawn up before an application for a permit is made in respect of demolition works exceeding the volume thresholds laid down by the regulations, those thresholds differing between non-residential and residential buildings. Where demolition is carried out under the supervision of a recognised demolition follow-up organisation and the corresponding certificate is obtained, the resulting rubble is accepted by a processor as having a low environmental risk profile, which permits the production of certified recycled granulates. Rubble not accompanied by such traceability is treated as having a high environmental risk profile, is processed separately and at materially higher cost, and may be refused. The company establishes before quoting whether a project falls above or below the applicable threshold and records which regime will apply. SELECTIVE DEMOLITION: waste streams are separated at source, which is determined by the sequence of operations and cannot be corrected at the point of removal. ASBESTOS: before works are undertaken an asbestos inventory of the workplace is required under the legislation on the welfare of workers, and this obligation is distinct from, and additional to, the asbestos certificate required in connection with the sale of a building constructed before 2001. Removal of asbestos by techniques other than those classified as simple operations is reserved to recognised asbestos removal undertakings; simple operations may be carried out only by trained personnel and only under the conditions prescribed. Where asbestos is identified and its removal is reserved, the company does not carry out that removal and the works are suspended until a recognised undertaking has acted.
